# **App Name**: الخليل

## Core Features:

- Curriculum Management: Admin interface for managing grades and lessons.
- Integrated Lesson Interface: A unified view for each lesson including video, summary, and quiz access.
- Automated Content Suggestions: AI tool that suggests relevant learning content (videos, summaries) to students based on their quiz performance, pinpointing areas of difficulty.
- Performance Analysis for Instructors: AI tool providing insights to instructors like الاستاذ خليل regarding student difficulties for specific lessons.
- Interactive Quizzes: Real-time, interactive quizzes to engage students and assess their understanding.
- Material Library: A dedicated section for adding and managing general resources not tied to a specific lesson.
- User Progress Tracking: Allows students to save progress and track results.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A calm, dependable blue (#4285F4) evoking knowledge and trust.
- Background color: A very light blue (#F0F4FF), almost white, to provide a clean, non-distracting backdrop.
- Accent color: A vibrant purple (#9C27B0) to highlight interactive elements and calls to action.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for a modern and accessible feel, suitable for both headlines and body text.
- A clean and intuitive design focusing on ease of navigation and quick access to essential resources, such as ClickUp and Shopify.
- Subtle transitions and animations to provide feedback on interactions and enhance user experience.
- Use of clear and modern icons to represent different sections and features.
